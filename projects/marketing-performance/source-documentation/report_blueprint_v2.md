# Report blueprint v2

## Shared design

Keep the current marketing palette and 1280×720 decision grid. Use the header/right categorical month slicer, left navigator with three filters, five KPIs, sparklines, and 2–3 analysis visuals per row.

## Page 1 — Bank Marketing

Decision: establish scale, conversion, channel mix, month context, and segment direction. Job ranking uses a 500-contact minimum; detail keeps Contacts and rate together.

## Page 2 — Audience Segmentation

Decision: compare job, age, marital, education, and loan/default groups. Job and age rate rankings use the 500-contact guardrail. Do not hide `unknown` silently.

## Page 3 — Campaign Effectiveness

Decision: compare channel, contact frequency, day/month context, and prior outcome. Duration is descriptive post-contact evidence only.

## Responsible-analysis rule

No chart title or annotation may claim that a segment/channel caused conversion. High-rate segments below 500 contacts belong in detail views with a sample-size flag, not in the headline ranking.
