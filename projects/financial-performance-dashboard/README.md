# Sports & Health Enterprise — Financial Performance

Power BI portfolio project for P&L performance, business-line economics, cost concentration, and break-even resilience.

> **Entity note:** “Sports & Health Enterprise” is a descriptive portfolio label because the underlying case does not provide a legal company name.

## Review in 60 seconds

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Management question | How can management move from a high-level P&L view to the drivers behind performance, segment contribution, and break-even dynamics? |
| What I built | A four-page Power BI report and a 12-slide interactive management story. |
| Evidence | A 2023 sports-and-health case model containing 580 financial transactions. |
| Status | Packaged PBIX and matching HTML story are published. |

## Goal

Build a finance control product that helps management:

- Compare margin across business lines.
- Monitor monthly Opex efficiency and quarterly EBIT scale.
- Identify concentrated COGS and Opex drivers.
- Test seasonal break-even resilience.
- Translate the P&L into explicit operating actions.

## Dashboard pages

| Page | Decision lens |
|---|---|
| Overview | Establish KPIs, monthly direction, and the executive P&L view. |
| Breakdown | Investigate the drivers behind headline performance. |
| Segments | Compare contribution and economics across business segments. |
| Breakeven | Review profitability thresholds and the relationship between Opex and EBIT. |
