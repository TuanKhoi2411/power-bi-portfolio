# Sports & Health Enterprise — Financial Performance

## Management question

How can management move from a high-level P&L view to the drivers behind performance, segment contribution, and break-even dynamics?

## Dashboard

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Power BI report | [`Sports_Health_Enterprise_Financial_Performance_Dashboard.pbix`](./Sports_Health_Enterprise_Financial_Performance_Dashboard.pbix) |
| Pages | Overview · Breakdown · Segments · Breakeven |
| Visual inventory | 73 visual containers across four report pages |
| Analytical lenses | P&L · revenue · net profit · segment contribution · break-even · Opex and EBIT |

The decision flow starts with executive KPIs, then moves into operating drivers, segment comparison, and profitability thresholds.

## Interactive presentation

[`Sports_Health_Enterprise_Financial_Performance_Interactive.html`](./Sports_Health_Enterprise_Financial_Performance_Interactive.html) is the matching 12-slide browser presentation. Download it and open locally in Chrome or Edge.

Controls: `←` / `→` or `Space` to navigate, `F` for fullscreen, and `O` for overview.

## Review path

1. Use **Overview** to establish overall direction.
2. Open **Breakdown** to investigate the drivers behind the headline result.
3. Compare business contribution through **Segments**.
4. Review profitability thresholds and the Opex–EBIT relationship in **Breakeven**.
5. Use the HTML presentation for the concise management narrative and the PBIX for interactive inspection.

## Verification and limits

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop is required to open the PBIX report.
- The repository packages the finished PBIX and its corresponding HTML story together.
- Confidential source details and claims that cannot be verified from the published report are intentionally excluded.
- A fresh Power BI Desktop reopen was not performed during this repository packaging step.
