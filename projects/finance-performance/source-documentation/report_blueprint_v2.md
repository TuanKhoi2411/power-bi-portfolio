# Report blueprint v2

## Revised decision flow

- **Executive Overview:** latest margin ladder, like-for-like quarterly revenue growth, leverage versus cash buffer, margin quality, and a quarterly CFO scorecard.
- **Profitability & Growth:** latest cost/profit rates, gross-to-net conversion, cost burden as a share of revenue, revenue growth versus net margin, and driver detail.
- **Balance & Liquidity:** net-asset cushion versus cash, current balance-sheet position, cash coverage of liabilities, capital and cash shares of assets, and risk detail.

## Shared design

Retain the existing 1280×720 finance grid and coherent executive palette: dark header, primary reporting-date slicer, left navigation/filters, five KPIs, compact trend sparklines, and a two-row analysis zone.

## Page 1 — Apple Finance

Decision: establish latest reported profitability and financial direction. Sparklines now use quarterly Revenue/Gross Profit/Operating Income/Net Income and period Gross Margin, so they show actual movement.

## Page 2 — Profitability & Growth

Decision: compare compatible quarterly flow facts, margins, and cost structure. Keep PeriodType and filing context visible; do not combine annual and quarterly flows.

## Page 3 — Balance & Liquidity

Decision: assess point-in-time assets, liabilities, cash, leverage, and cash-to-assets. KPI 5 and the companion trend now show Cash to Assets; Cash Coverage of Liabilities remains available in the detail table.

## Integrity rule

Never sum balance-sheet facts across dates, never present latest filing facts as forecasts, and keep form/filed-date/frame/accession traceability available.
